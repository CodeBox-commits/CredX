ANNUAL REPORT 2024-25
Nebula Components Private Limited
Statement of Profit and Loss
Board of Directors report

Revenue for FY25 was INR 186 crore.
Working capital remained stretched in Q4.
Liquidity pressure increased because receivables remained overdue for more than 90 days.
The company disclosed related party purchases from promoter-linked group companies.
Management is evaluating restructuring of unsecured promoter support.
The credit rating outlook was revised to negative after margin compression.
Auditor's report included an emphasis of matter regarding going concern assumptions.
